MHSSZ VR – youth competitors, 2018–2026

Source: vr.mhssz.hu public results: every Magyar Kupa / MB round in the system (boulder, lead, speed) from 2018, when the system started, to today, plus 4 off-calendar youth events: the 2024 Gerecse amateur boulder, the 2025 and 2026 selection events, and the 2026 U13/U15 Regional Boulder Cup in Sukoró (date approximate). The “Include non-MK events” checkbox toggles those 4, downloaded . Performance = percentile within the category field: 100 = won, 0 = last (place p of N → 100·(N−p)/(N−1)). Places: in boulder and lead, whoever reached a final (döntő, szuperdöntő) is ranked by that round first, with ties going to the earlier round. The VR system ranks some categories, mainly older ifi and junior climbers in an open final, by qualification only. Speed keeps the VR order. In addition, 2026 boulder rounds are re-ranked with the points system in their rules (top 25, zone 10, −0.1 per failed attempt; in finals, ties go to the qualification result), because the VR system's own ranking still orders by tops, zones and attempts. Birth year is inferred from the 2-year age bands. From 2025: U9 szupermini, U11 mini, U13 gyerek, U15 serdülő, U17 ifi B, U19 ifi A, U21 junior (2026: serdülő = 2012/13). Up to 2024 the bands were one year older (U10, U12, U14…; 2024 serdülő = 2011/12), and the data confirms it. Competing in two consecutive seasons usually pins the exact year (not 2024→25, whose bands pair the same years). Amateur, adult and senior fields are excluded, and U9 is unranked.

Magyar Kupa season standings

Calculated with the MHSSZ rulebook (2023, §10) from the corrected places. Points per round: 100, 80, 65, 55, 51, 47, 43, 40, 37, 34 … down to 1 point for 30th, and tied places share the average. Only climbers with a valid sports medical earn points; foreign guests and anyone without one are skipped, and those below move up. 4–5 rounds: the weakest is dropped; 6 or more: the weakest two are dropped. Struck-through numbers are dropped rounds. Title condition: missed at most 2 rounds and took part in at least half. The same method reproduces 322 of 325 of the system's published 2025 totals. The 2026 rules document isn't public, so 2026 assumes the 2023 system.
